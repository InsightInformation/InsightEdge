# Service Agreement Template

A simple one-page agreement to use with every client. Copy it into a Google Doc, fill in the brackets, and have both people sign (a typed name in an email reply saying "I agree" works for small jobs). **Have a local attorney review it once** before regular use. Many offer flat-fee small-business document reviews.

---

**YOUR BACKUP PLAN, SERVICE AGREEMENT**

**Provider:** Your Backup Plan [LLC], Ashley Harris, 412-401-3208, yourbackupplan.ashley@gmail.com
**Client:** [Name / Business] · [Address] · [Phone] · [Email]
**Date:** [ ]

**1. Services.** Provider will perform the following: [e.g., front desk and phone coverage, data entry, event setup].
Location: [ ] · Dates / schedule: [ ] · Point of contact at Client: [ ]

**2. Rates.** $[35] per hour, [3]-hour minimum per visit. / Flat project fee of $[ ].
Same-day requests (under 24 hours' notice): +$[10]/hour. Mileage for errands during the job at the current IRS standard rate. Pre-approved supplies purchased for Client are reimbursed at cost with receipts.

**3. Invoicing & payment.** Provider invoices [weekly / at completion]. Payment due within [15] days by check, Zelle, Venmo Business, or card. Balances more than 30 days past due may incur a 1.5% monthly late fee.

**4. Cancellations.** Client may cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice at no charge. Cancellations with less notice are billed at the minimum visit charge. If Provider must cancel (illness, emergency), Provider will notify Client as soon as possible and there is no charge.

**5. Independent business.** Provider is an independent business, not an employee of Client. Provider is responsible for its own taxes, insurance and equipment and controls the manner and means of performing the work. Provider may serve other clients. Client will not provide employee benefits or withhold taxes.

**6. Confidentiality.** Provider will keep confidential all non-public information about Client, its customers, students, patients and records, both during and after the engagement, and will follow Client's privacy policies (including HIPAA or FERPA procedures where applicable) as directed. Provider will return or destroy Client materials at the end of the engagement.

**7. Client responsibilities.** Client will provide access, instructions, necessary equipment and system logins, and a safe workspace. Client remains responsible for decisions, approvals, and the final accuracy of its own financial, legal and medical records.

**8. Limitation of liability.** Provider will perform services in a professional manner. Provider's total liability for any claim relating to this agreement is limited to the fees paid for the services giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

**9. Direct hire.** If Client wishes to hire Provider directly as an employee, the parties will discuss this in good faith. *(Optional line; delete if not wanted.)*

**10. Term.** This agreement covers the services above and any future services Client requests unless either party ends it with written notice. Pennsylvania law applies.

**Provider signature:** ____________________ Date: ________

## New Client Intake Checklist (use on the first call)

- [ ] Contact name, business, phone, email
- [ ] What needs to get done? What does "done" look like?
- [ ] Dates, hours, start time, and how long it's likely to last
- [ ] Where to park, where to go, who to ask for
- [ ] Dress code
- [ ] Systems / logins needed (phones, scheduling software, email)
- [ ] Confidentiality needs (patients, students, client files)
- [ ] Who approves the hours / receives the invoice
- [ ] Do they need a W-9, insurance certificate or clearances before day one?
- [ ] Quote given: $______ Confirmed by: email / text / signed agreement
