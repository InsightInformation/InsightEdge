# First 90 Days Launch Plan

Goal: **3–5 repeat clients** who call her whenever they're short-handed. One good office client can easily be worth several thousand dollars a year.

## Week 1: Get set up
- [ ] Create the Gmail `yourbackupplan.ashley@gmail.com` (if not done) and a Google Voice number *(optional: keeps the business line separate from her personal cell)*
- [ ] Facebook business page (`launch-posts.md` #1), with the flyer as the cover image
- [ ] Google Business Profile (`launch-posts.md` #4). Google verification can take a few days, so start early
- [ ] Put the website online (see the main README)
- [ ] Order business cards (Vistaprint or a local print shop; about $20–30 for 250). Card text is at the bottom of this file
- [ ] Print 50 flyers
- [ ] Open a separate checking account for the business (see `../business/launch-checklist.md`)

## Week 2: Launch
- [ ] Ashley posts her personal launch story + flyer
- [ ] Share to the business page, pin post #3
- [ ] Text 20–30 close friends and family asking them to share (`outreach-scripts.md` #5)
- [ ] Post on Nextdoor and 3–5 local Facebook groups (following group rules)
- [ ] LinkedIn update

## Weeks 3–4: Direct outreach
- [ ] List 40 local targets (offices, schools, churches, shops) within about 20 minutes of home
- [ ] Email or walk in to **10 per week**
- [ ] Email former school colleagues, especially office staff at other buildings and districts
- [ ] Join the local chamber: **South Hills Chamber of Commerce** or **Washington County Chamber of Commerce**. Their networking events are full of small-business owners who are exactly her customers
- [ ] Ask the local library, coffee shops and community centers if she can pin a flyer

## Month 2: Deliver and gather proof
- [ ] Every job: send the thank-you and review request (`outreach-scripts.md` #6)
- [ ] Goal: **5 Google reviews**
- [ ] Follow up with everyone contacted in weeks 3–4
- [ ] Post 2–3 times a week on Facebook (ideas in `launch-posts.md`)
- [ ] Track every lead in a simple spreadsheet: name, business, date contacted, follow-up date, result

## Month 3: Build repeat business
- [ ] Check in with past clients: "Anything coming up I can help with?"
- [ ] Offer a **"backup on call"** plan to 1–2 favorite clients (see `../business/pricing.md`)
- [ ] Look at what's working (where did clients come from?) and do more of that
- [ ] Plan for seasonal peaks: tax season (Jan–Apr) for accountants, back-to-school (Aug), holidays (Nov–Dec) for retail and events
