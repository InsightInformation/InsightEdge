Looking back at every job I've ever had, the part I've loved most has always been the same: helping people. I love being the person someone can count on. I love solving problems, organizing the chaos, figuring things out, and jumping in when something needs done.

Common Questions

What areas do you serve?

The South Hills of Pittsburgh and Washington County, including communities like Bethel Park, Mt. Lebanon, Upper St. Clair, South Park, Peters Township, Canonsburg, Bridgeville, Castle Shannon, Whitehall, Baldwin, Pleasant Hills, McMurray and Washington. Close by but not on the list? Just ask.

How much notice do you need?

The more the better, but call-offs happen. Reach out as soon as you know, and if I'm available I'll do everything I can to help, including same-day and next-day coverage.

Is there a minimum or a contract?

No long-term commitment. Work is booked by the hour, day, week or project, with a short minimum per visit. You'll get a clear quote before any work begins.

Do I have to hire you as an employee?

No. Your Backup Plan is an independent business. You don't take on payroll, benefits or hiring paperwork. You just get a simple invoice for the time worked.

Can you help in schools?

Yes. With a background working in a high school, I'm comfortable in school offices and around students, staff and families, and I can provide current Pennsylvania school clearances (PA State Police, Child Abuse History and FBI fingerprint) on request.

Will you keep our information confidential?

Always. Discretion is part of the job, and I'm glad to sign your confidentiality agreement.
